# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis & Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes pizza sales data using **SQL and Power BI** to identify key sales trends, customer ordering patterns, top-performing pizzas, and underperforming products.

The project includes SQL queries for data analysis and an interactive **Power BI dashboard** for visualizing the results and generating business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall pizza sales performance
- Calculate important business KPIs
- Identify daily and monthly order trends
- Analyze sales by pizza category and size
- Identify top and bottom-performing pizzas
- Compare pizzas based on revenue, quantity sold, and total orders
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for better data visualization

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the Power BI dashboard:

- **Friday** has the highest number of orders among the days of the week.
- Orders are generally higher toward the **weekend**, particularly Friday and Saturday.
- **July** records the highest monthly number of orders.
- The **Classic** pizza category contributes the highest share of sales.
- **Large-size pizzas** contribute the highest share of sales.
- The **Classic category** has the highest number of pizzas sold.
- **The Thai Chicken Pizza** generates the highest revenue among the pizzas.
- **The Classic Deluxe Pizza** has the highest total quantity sold and total orders.
- **The Brie Carre Pizza** appears among the lowest-performing pizzas in revenue, quantity, and total orders.
